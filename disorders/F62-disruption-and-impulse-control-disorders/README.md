# F62 · Disruption and Impulse-Control Disorders

Disorders of action under threat. The patient cannot refrain from a move that relieves distress in the short term at the expense of its position.

Disorders added to this category by local editions take codes ending in `.8`, e.g. `F62.8x`. To propose a disorder for this edition, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
