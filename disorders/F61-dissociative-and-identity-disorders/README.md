# F61 · Dissociative and Identity Disorders

Disorders of the patient’s account of what it is. The patient’s stated identity changes faster than its products, customers or capabilities, and its statements and actions come apart.

This category is open. No disorder has yet been admitted. Disorders added to this category by local editions take codes ending in `.8`, e.g. `F61.8x`. To propose a disorder for this edition, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
