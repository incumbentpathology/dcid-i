# Contributing

Guidance for attending physicians.

## 1. The patient is a type

Record every patient by category: “an issue-tracker incumbent,” “a document-system founder.” Do not name the company, its people or its products, and do not quote a statement exactly enough that a search finds its author. The reader should recognize the pattern from what a whole category is saying, not identify a particular patient. Submissions that name a company will be closed.

## 2. Reporting a case

A case report follows the form of a clinical case report: patient information, presenting complaint, history, timeline, mental status examination, diagnostic assessment against the criteria, intervention, outcome and discussion. Copy [`cases/_template.md`](cases/_template.md); [`cases/2026-11-six-weeks-of-cash.md`](cases/2026-11-six-weeks-of-cash.md) is a completed example.

Every finding code you assign must name the criteria it meets and the evidence for each. Mark the doctor’s interpretation as an inference. If you cannot complete a pull request, the *Report a case* issue form collects the same sections.

## 3. Proposing a disorder

A disorder follows the structure of the existing entries: diagnostic criteria, specifiers, recording procedures, diagnostic and associated features, prevalence, development and course, risk and prognostic factors, differential diagnosis and comorbidity. A proposal needs at least the criteria, the differential diagnosis and one case. Open a *Propose a disorder* issue. The editors assign codes and the category; please do not pick your own outside `.8` (see the README on forks).

## 4. Register

Clinical and deadpan. The doctor does not joke and does not raise his voice. One plain sentence per sign.
