# Contributing

Guidance for attending physicians.

## 1. The patient is a type

Record every patient by category: “an issue-tracker incumbent,” “a document-system founder.” Do not name the company, its people or its products, and do not quote a statement exactly enough that a search finds its author. The reader should recognize the pattern from what a whole category is saying, not identify a particular patient. Submissions that name a company will be closed.

## 2. Reporting a case

A case cites one or more finding codes. Record:

- **Date.** When the sign was observed, to the month.
- **Patient.** The type, per §1.
- **Findings.** The codes, e.g. `R67.1, R67.4`.
- **Observed.** What the patient said or did, paraphrased.
- **Inference.** The doctor’s interpretation, marked as such.

Use [`cases/_template.md`](cases/_template.md), or the *Report a case* issue form.

## 3. Proposing a condition

A condition needs a name, a sign or working definition, and at least one case note. Open a *Propose a condition* issue. The editors assign codes in R67; please do not pick your own outside `.8` (see the README on forks).

## 4. Register

Clinical and deadpan. The doctor does not joke and does not raise his voice. One plain sentence per sign.
