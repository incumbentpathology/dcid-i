# Six weeks of cash

**Findings:** R67.3 · Hail Mary, commercial type, runway 6 weeks. R67.1 · Copium magnificum, insight unknown.

## 1. Patient Information

- **Category:** a workflow-software incumbent
- **Age:** 10–20 years since founding
- **Size:** 500–5,000
- **Ownership:** venture-backed, last priced round more than three years ago
- **Presenting through:** consultation

## 2. Presenting Complaint

Patient reports six weeks of cash and one prospective enterprise customer.

## 3. History of Present Illness

Growth slowed over the preceding six quarters. Patient attributes the slowdown to longer enterprise sales cycles. Two challengers in the category raised rounds in the same period.

## 4. Past History

One rebrand and one reorganization in the past eighteen months. An assistant was added to the product in the prior year. No prior Hail Mary.

## 5. Timeline

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Six quarters prior | Growth begins to slow | Patient report |
| Eighteen months prior | Rebrand; reorganization | Patient report |
| Three months prior | Sales team reassigned to a single prospective customer | Patient report |
| At consultation | Six weeks of cash remaining | Patient report |

## 6. Mental Status Examination

- **Appearance:** Recently rebranded.
- **Behavior:** Few announcements in the past quarter. Calm.
- **Speech:** Frequent use of *transformational* and *strategic partner*.
- **Mood:** “Confident.”
- **Affect:** Restricted.
- **Thought process:** Coherent.
- **Thought content:** Fixed belief that the prospective customer will sign within the runway.
- **Orientation:** Oriented to the challenger. Not oriented to procurement timelines.
- **Insight:** Unknown.
- **Judgment:** Poor, as shown by the absence of a plan for the case in which the customer does not sign.

## 7. Diagnostic Assessment

**R67.3 · Hail Mary.** Criterion A met: the sales team and the remaining cash are committed to one customer. Criterion B met: six weeks is shorter than a typical enterprise procurement cycle. Criterion C met: patient declined to discuss an alternative. Criterion D met: patient described the deal as “a matter of conviction.”

**R67.1 · Copium magnificum.** Criteria A3 and A6 met. Insight cannot be assigned from the consultation alone.

**Differential.** Focus excluded: the patient cannot survive failure. Strategic convulsions excluded: resources are concentrated, not dispersed.

## 8. Intervention

Asked how long procurement usually takes, patient requests that the customer’s name be withheld until the deal closes.

## 9. Follow-up and Outcomes

Pending. The outcome is due within six weeks of consultation.

## 10. Discussion

**Inference.** The request to withhold the customer’s name answers the question about procurement.

## 11. Sources

Illustrative case. No patient was harmed in its preparation.
