## 1. Patient Information

Record the patient by type, never by name. Use bands, not exact figures.

- **Category:** e.g. an issue-tracker incumbent
- **Age:** years since founding, in bands (5–10, 10–20, 20+)
- **Size:** headcount band (under 500, 500–5,000, over 5,000)
- **Ownership:** public, private equity, or venture-backed
- **Presenting through:** earnings call, keynote, press release, post, interview

## 2. Presenting Complaint

One or two sentences: what the patient said or did that brought it to attention.

## 3. History of Present Illness

## 4. Past History

## 6. Mental Status Examination

- **Appearance:** branding, recent rebrands, presentation materials
- **Behavior:** rate and pattern of announcements
- **Speech:** characteristic vocabulary
- **Mood:** as the patient describes it
- **Affect:** as the clinician observes it
- **Thought process:** coherent, tangential, or contradictory
- **Thought content:** recurring reassurances and fixed beliefs
- **Orientation:** to the current market, to the challenger, to time
- **Insight:** good or fair, poor, absent, or unknown
- **Judgment:** as shown in recent decisions

## 7. Diagnostic Assessment
